How the samplers are tested

A sampler can run, produce plausible points and still be wrong. The test suite checks every sampler against answers known in advance, and against the error bars it reports.

Calibration

For each sampler and test polytope, means and standard deviations are compared with their exact values in units of the Monte Carlo standard errors from diagnostics. For a correct sampler with honest error bars these z-scores have root mean square near 1. Over 20 to 40 seeds on a pentagon, cubes, a skinny cube and simplices:

SamplerRMS of z-scores
BilliardWalk, CoordinateHitAndRun, HitAndRun, ACHR0.89–1.11
DikinWalk, VaidyaWalk0.94–1.11
CRHMC0.71–1.17
independent draws, for reference1.00–1.02

The same check on non-uniform targets (Dirichlet, truncated exponentials, a correlated Gaussian) gave 0.73–1.15 for every supported sampler.

Test suites

  • test/ (just ci): exact moments on squares, simplices, a pentagon and cubes; checks adapted from volesti, hopsy, hops and PolyRound; reproducibility; type stability and zero allocations of every step (JET and AllocCheck); gradients against finite differences.
  • test/stress.jl (just stress): R̂ flags chains started near corners; runs doubled until R̂ < 1.01 give correct moments on a box of aspect ratio 10⁶, 560 redundant constraints, implicit equalities, a 256-facet cross-polytope, the Birkhoff polytope and a 200-dimensional simplex; 100 000 steps of every sampler stay inside four polytopes; the growth of mixing cost with dimension matches the literature.

Cross-checks

  • hopsy 1.7, on the same polytopes: estimates agree within their standard errors, and CHRR effective samples per move are within a factor 1.1 to 1.7, explained by hopsy's cyclic coordinate order and its rounding basis.
  • Two converged samplers with different mechanisms agree on polytopes with no exact answer.

Problems the tests found

  • Output bundling copied the sample matrix once per point (quadratic in the number of samples).
  • A first CRHMC integrator that reused the start-of-step factorization failed to converge on 2% of steps, mostly near facets; the resulting rejections biased standard deviations 4 standard errors low. Refactoring during slow integrations removed the bias.
  • polytopewalk draws its Dikin and Vaidya proposals with the lower Cholesky factor, which has the wrong covariance for its Metropolis ratio; on a pentagon that version put means 9 to 13 standard errors from the truth.
  • A boundedness check based only on Stiemke's theorem missed unbounded strips such as 0 ≤ x₁ - x₂ ≤ 1; it now also requires full column rank.
